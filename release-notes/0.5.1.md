## JDex Manager 0.5.1

Obsidian plugin and external Hebra plugin, same release.

### Obsidian
- "Date files when created" now only dates files created on this device and opened right away (within 2 s). Files that arrive through Obsidian Sync are no longer renamed: their creation time on the receiving device is their arrival (measured), so they were dated with the wrong day and renamed at the same time as the sender.
- Files created here that are not opened (dropped attachments, files written by other plugins) are left undated.

### Hebra
- No changes.
